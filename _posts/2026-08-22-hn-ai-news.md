    points: 104

    comments: 18
    time: Aug 21, 16:46 UTC
    content_bullets:

    - 'Cache latencies measured on RTX 4090: L1 hit ~15 ns (40 cycles), L2 hit ~127 ns (330 cycles), main DRAM ~255 ns (660 cycles).'
    - L1 is virtually addressed and 4-way set-associative; L2 is physically indexed, 16-way, split across 36 independent 2 MiB slices with address-dependent routing.
    - DRAM access requires opening an expensive row buffer (activate) before cheaper column reads — non-coalesced access multiplies these costly row activations.
    - The SM scheduler hides per-load latency by swapping in other runnable warps while one stalls, making throughput — not single-load speed — the real metric.
