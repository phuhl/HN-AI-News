          - "PrismML's Bonsai is an ultra-compressed 1-bit/ternary neural net — weights are just -1, 0, or +1 — making DRAM's majority-of-3 charge operation sufficient to run full model inference in-place."
          - "Real-silicon benchmarks on commodity DDR4 DIMMs showed 13.3x speedup: 632 seconds down to 47.5 seconds per token for a 2.4B-parameter model, with Bonsai-1.7B producing token-exact matches against CPU output."
          - "The key advantage is zero data movement: weights live in DRAM and compute there, eliminating the memory bus bottleneck that causes thermal throttling and energy drain during normal AI inference."
